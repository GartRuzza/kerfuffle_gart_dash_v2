# TRF Constitution (TRUFFLE / KERFUFFLE)

*by your Commissioners Max*

## Introduction

This is the League Constitution of The Radical Ultimate Fantasy Football League Experience (*TRUFFLE*) & Kinetically Evolved Radically Fantastic Ultimate Fantasy Football League Experience (*KERFUFFLE*), collectively referred to as **TRF** (Truly Ridiculous Fantasy) — the world's most complicated 12-team dynasty league that makes days at work just a bit less boring, and keeps this group of owners, friends, family, friends of family, and family of friends in touch.

The Constitution intends to capture all TRUFFLE rules and regulations, from scheduling, to roster management, to all the confusing salary cap stuff.

## Roster Rules

### Starting Lineup

As a Superflex league, the starting lineup composition is as follows (sorry Kickers!):

- **QB**
- **RB**
- **RB**
- **WR**
- **WR**
- **TE**
- **FLEX** — RB/WR/TE
- **FLEX** — RB/WR/TE
- **SFLEX** — QB/RB/WR/TE
- **D/ST**

*Note:* Tanking teams may not intentionally start injured players, players on Bye Weeks, or backup Quarterbacks, and must field a valid and active starting lineup.

### Roster Composition

Beyond 10 starters, each team may roster **9 bench players** with no positional restrictions, and there are **2 Injured Reserve (IR) spots** that players may be moved to while they are eligible to be placed on IR on CBS. Teams **may roster fewer than the default 19 players** (not including IR), but must always have enough players to field a legal starting lineup.

## Salary Cap & Transactions

### Salary Cap

Each team has a **$500 salary cap**, meaning the sum of player salaries on a team's roster (**including players on IR**) may not exceed $500.

### Player Salaries

Each player is assigned a salary value either when:

a) they are drafted (based on Rookie Draft salary scale or Rookie Extension),
b) won during the offseason Free Agent auction (winning auction bid),
c) won during weekly in-season Waiver/FAB runs (winning FAB bid), or
d) **franchise tagged**.

That salary remains in effect until a player's contract expires or the player is cut (at which point a Dead Cap Penalty may be incurred).

### Player Contracts

Along with their salaries, each player has a contract duration for which their TRUFFLE team owns them at the determined salary value. **Player contracts may be 1, 2, 3, or 4 years in length.**

Each player is assigned a contract duration under the same four circumstances listed above. Contract lengths are determined as follows:

1. **Drafted players** automatically receive **3-year contracts**; **Rookie extensions** automatically add **2 years of contract**.
2. Owners assign **1-, 2-, 3-, or 4-year contracts to their won free agents** within a week of the Free Agent auction.
3. Owners may assign **1- or 2-year contracts to Waiver/FAB free agents** picked up in-season.
4. **Franchise tagged players** automatically receive **1 additional year of contract**.

### Dead Cap Policies

While Owners have control over their players at the fixed salary and contract values, they may cut player contracts under the following rules:

- Players with **1 year** remaining on their contract can be cut at all times without any cap penalty.
- Players with **2 years** remaining **AND a salary of $15 or less** can be cut at all times without any cap penalty.
- Players with **2 years** remaining **AND a salary of more than $15** can be cut, but will result in a dead salary cap hit of **25% of their salary for 1 year**.
- Players with **3 years** remaining can be cut, but will result in a dead salary cap hit of **50% of their salary for 2 years**.
- Players with **4 years** remaining can be cut, but will result in a dead salary cap hit of **75% of their salary for 3 years**.

**Sh\*\* Happens Addendum**

- Players facing significant NFL suspensions for extenuating off-field circumstances (as determined by the commissioner and TRUFFLE owners), who would typically incur a dead cap penalty, may be cut but only result in a dead salary cap hit of **10% of their salary for 1 year**.

All dead cap values are rounded up, and are reflected on CBS as inactive players that the Commissioner manually adds to the roster.

**Dead cap may not be included in trades** for another team to take on dead salary.

### Waivers (FAB)

*[Modified 2024 to include **Playoff Waiver Runs**]*

During the season, players not on rosters will always be on waivers and available for FAB (Free Agent Bidding) per CBS terminology. To pick up an available player, all teams may bid on them from their remaining salary cap space ($500 − sum of team salaries), and the **winning bid will become the player's salary**. **Owners are not allowed to bid on and win back players they had previously dropped at a lower salary value during the next waiver run.**

Waivers/FAB will run twice a week during the season: **Wednesday nights before Thursday night football**, and **Saturday night before Sunday games**. Waivers/FAB will typically begin about 1 week before the NFL season, and stop upon conclusion of the Bench Cup and fantasy season. Players may be dropped in accordance with Dead Cap policies during the offseason. **During the Fantasy Playoffs there will be additional waiver runs for Monday night games and Wednesday night** (instead of the Thursday waiver run).

Players picked up on Waivers may be given either a 1- or 2-year contract. Given no Dead Cap penalty for players on 2-year contracts ≤ $15, the default Waiver contract will be 2 years, **but when the winning bid exceeds $15 the commissioner will check in with the corresponding owner(s) for them to officially decide contract length**.

### Trades

Trades in TRUFFLE may involve **players**, **Rookie Draft Picks from the current season + the next 3 seasons**, and **Dead Cap values**. Teams may not trade protected draft picks. Trades may be executed in-season before the **trade deadline** that typically **occurs around Thanksgiving**. Upon conclusion of the Bench Cup and fantasy season, trades may occur at any point during the offseason. Trades may be executed during regular season weeks except Sundays, Mondays, or if players involved have already played.

Player contracts and salaries remain unchanged by trades, **but both teams must be at or below the $500 salary cap upon completion of the trade**. The Trade Machine on TRUFFLEdash (https://rmaxsm.shinyapps.io/truffledash/) can be used as a tool to solve trade salary implications.

### Franchise Tag

When a non-rookie player contract expires at season's end, the player automatically becomes a Free Agent and part of the player pool for the Free Agent Auction, **unless the player is Franchise Tagged**.

Similar to the NFL, a **Franchise Tag** gives owners the option to automatically extend a player's contract by 1 year at a **positionally predetermined Franchise Tag salary**. Each offseason, **any team may franchise tag up to 2 players**. Any **individual player may be franchise tagged 2 times** before automatically entering Free Agency.

The positionally predetermined Franchise Tag salary is calculated as follows:

- If the player **has not been franchise tagged before**, his additional 1-year contract salary becomes the **average salary (rounded up) of the top 5 highest paid players at his position** from the previous season.
- If the player **has not been franchise tagged before** AND his **previous salary exceeded that top-5 average (rounded up)**, his additional 1-year contract salary becomes **$1 more than his previous year salary**.
- If the player **was franchise tagged the previous year**, his additional 1-year contract salary becomes **$1 more than the previous highest salary in the league at his position**.

## Offseason Events

### Rookie Draft

*[Modified 2024 to include **Tanking Fix Optimal Lineup Points Solution**]*

The **Rookie Draft** will be the first of two annual offseason events and serve as the primary manner through which first-year NFL players will enter the TRUFFLE player pool. It will typically occur about a week or two after the NFL draft. The first pick is determined by the **Rookie Draft Lottery**, which assigns odds by inverse order of **regular season optimal lineup points** and playoff results, and provides a 15% odds boost to the consolation bracket winner.

Optimal lineup points are calculated week-by-week for every team based on the best-ball-style 1 QB / 2 RB / 2 WR / 1 TE / 1 FLX / 1 SFLX lineup and will be tracked through TRUFFLEdash directly.

**Rookie Draft Lottery Odds**

| Regular Season Finish | Odds |
| --- | --- |
| Last | 30% |
| 11th | 22.5% |
| 10th | 15% |
| 9th | 7.5% |
| 8th | 5% |
| 7th | 5% |
| Consolation Winner | +15% |

**Final draft order**

1. Rookie Draft **Lottery Winner**
2. **(2–6)** **Non-Playoff teams** minus lottery winner, *in inverse order of possible optimal lineup points*
3. **(7–8)** Teams **eliminated in the first round** of playoffs, *in inverse order of possible optimal lineup points*
4. **(9–10)** Teams **eliminated in the semi-finals** of playoffs, *in inverse order of possible optimal lineup points*
5. **(11)** League **Runner Up**
6. **(12)** League **Champion**

**All draft picks** are automatically given **3-year contracts**, or otherwise go unsigned and enter the Free Agent Auction Pool.

**KERFUFFLE 3rd round picks rule — 2025/26 addition**

Under KERFUFFLE rules, one 3rd round pick can be placed on the Practice Squad in the offseason prior to their rookie season. This player does not count towards active roster limits but does count towards the salary cap. The player can remain on the Practice Squad until the end of their rookie season but cannot return to the Practice Squad once activated. This player can be traded, and the receiving team can keep them on the Practice Squad, provided they do not already have a 3rd round pick occupying the Practice Squad position.

### Rookie Extensions

At the end of their 3-year rookie contracts, all originally drafted players that were not cut are eligible for 2-year rookie contract extensions. Players that were traded during their rookie contracts are still eligible for extensions with the team they are on when their rookie contract expires.

Rookie extension values are determined by a fixed scale factoring in player position and original draft slot:

| Draft Pick ↓ / Position → | QB | RB | WR | TE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1.1 – 1.6** | $70 | $70 | $60 | $30 |
| **1.7 – 1.12** | $60 | $60 | $50 | $20 |
| **2** | $50 | $50 | $40 | $10 |
| **3** | $30 | $30 | $30 | $5 |

### Free Agent Auction

The second annual offseason event is the Free Agent Auction. It will typically occur during the month before the NFL season starts. At the end of each season, all players whose contracts expire, or are cut by their TRUFFLE team, become eligible for the free agency auction.

The auction is held on the CBS website. The nomination order for the auction is randomized, and teams take turns nominating eligible free agents for bidding. Each team has an auction budget equivalent to its available salary cap space ($500 − sum of team salaries), and may use that budget to bid on players in the auction.

Winning bids for each player become the player's salary, and Owners have 1 week from the end of the auction to assign either 1-, 2-, 3-, or 4-year contracts to their acquired free agents.

### Regular Season Schedule

Over the 14-game Regular Season, every team plays its 2 divisional opponents twice (4 games), every other cross-divisional opponent once (9 games), and plays 1 protected cross-divisional rivalry game annually on Thanksgiving.

### Standings

Standings are considered at a divisional and overall level. Standing tiebreakers between teams with identical records, in order:

1. Head-to-Head Record
2. Division Record
3. Total Points

### End-of-Season Competitions

Upon completion of the 14-week Regular Season, there are three distinct end-of-season competitions: **Playoffs**, **Consolation**, and the **Bench Cup**.

#### Playoffs

6 seeded teams qualify for the playoffs, which run from Week 15–17. The top 4 seeds are awarded to the 4 division champions, in order of best-to-worst record using standings tiebreakers. The 2 division champs with the best records receive Week 15 Byes in the first week of the playoffs. The 5th and 6th seeded Wild Card teams are the two non-division champions with the best record, using standings tiebreakers.

The winner of the playoffs wins the **Richard F. Smith Cup**, including annual engraving. There is also a third-place game for bragging rights.

#### Consolation Bracket

The 6 non-playoff teams are seeded into the Consolation bracket that runs from Week 15–17, which offers the winning team a 15% odds boost for the 1st Rookie Draft Pick Lottery. Seeding is awarded by inverse-standings order (**not optimal lineup points**), meaning the two worst teams in the league receive the first round Byes in the consolation bracket.

Additionally, the last place regular season team pays for the Championship Trophy engraving and shipping.

#### Bench Cup

The Bench Cup essentially runs separately from the rest of TRUFFLE and even the CBS website, with its results tracked through the TRUFFLE Command Center (Google Sheet) and hopefully TRUFFLEdash at some point. The tournament features all 12 TRUFFLE teams, randomly seeded into a 12-team, 4-round, 4-Bye bracket that runs from Week 15–18, and scoring is determined entirely by the benches of every team. The 4 Byes are awarded to the 4 Bye recipients from across the Playoffs and Consolation Bracket, so that those teams cannot stack their benches while they are off in Week 15.

During the Bench Cup, Consolation, and Playoffs, starting lineup restrictions regarding not playing injured players are lifted, since owners may decide which event to prioritize. Team benches also have no positional restrictions.

## Scoring System

*[Modified 2024 to include **Turnover on Downs**]*

TRUFFLE is a PPFD league, meaning players are awarded a point for receiving and rushing first downs. Beyond that, the scoring system is as follows.

### Offensive

**Passing**
- **PaYd:** 0.04 pts per Passing Yard
- **PaTD:** 4 pts per Passing Touchdown
- **PaInt:** −2 pts per Passing Interception
- **Pa2P:** 2 pts per Passing Two-Point Conversion

**Rushing**
- **RuYd:** 0.1 pts per Rushing Yard
- **RuTD:** 6 pts per Rushing Touchdown
- **RuFD:** 1 pt per Rushing First Down
- **Ru2P:** 2 pts per Rushing Two-Point Conversion

**Receiving**
- **ReYd:** 0.1 pts per Receiving Yard
- **ReTD:** 6 pts per Receiving Touchdown
- **ReFD:** 1 pt per Receiving First Down
- **Re2P:** 2 pts per Receiving Two-Point Conversion

**Miscellaneous**
- **FL:** −2 pts per Fumble Lost, including ST plays
- **Fum2PT:** Fumble Recovery Two-Point Conversion
- **OFRTD:** Offensive Fumble Recovery Touchdown

### Defensive / Special Teams

- **DFR:** 2 pts per Defensive/ST Fumble Recovered
- **DTD:** 6 pts per Defensive/ST Touchdown
- **Int:** 3 pts per Interception
- **SCK:** 1 pt per Sack
- **TOD:** 1 pt per Turnover on Downs
- **STY:** 2 pts per Safety
- **ST2PT:** 2 pts per Special Teams Conversion Return for Two Points
- **STY1PT:** 1 pt per Special Teams One-point Safety
- **PA:** Points Against, Total Points Scored:
  - 10 pts for 0 Points Against allowed
  - 7 pts for 1–6 Points Against allowed
  - 4 pts for 7–13 Points Against allowed
  - 1 pt for 14–20 Points Against allowed
  - 0 pts for 21–27 Points Against allowed
  - −1 pt for 28–35 Points Against allowed
  - −4 pts for 36+ Points Against allowed

- Championship Winner: $600
